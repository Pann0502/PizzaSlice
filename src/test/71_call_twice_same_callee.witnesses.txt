count: 2
W1: enter(foo@C1) -> branch(B1=T) -> target(T)
W2: enter(foo@C2) -> branch(B1=T) -> target(T)

notes:
  - The two witnesses share the same in-callee branch but differ by call site.
  - Without the call-site (function-enter) waypoint the two are indistinguishable
    and the split is not truly complete-with-context. This is the reason the
    draft calls out this case as the motivation for witness-based splitting.
  - In W2, the first call (C1) returns without error. A full witness may also
    need to express "foo at C1 took B1=F" to be concrete. Open question: does
    the tool pin the earlier non-erroring call, or leave it free? I left it
    free. Flag if you want it pinned.
